Instructional Design: Course Notes

The ADDIE model
ADDIE stands for Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation and Evaluation. It is a systematic process for creating learning experiences.

Learner and needs analysis
Start by finding out who the learners are, what they already know and what gap the course should close. Use short surveys or interviews.

Writing learning objectives
Good objectives describe observable behaviour, the conditions and the criteria for success, for example: "Given a dataset, the learner will create a bar chart with labelled axes."

Formative evaluation
Test early versions with real learners. Usability tests and expert reviews reveal problems before the course is finished.
